CLASSIFICATION: ALL STAFF
Q: What time do stores open and close?
A: Most stores trade 7am to 10pm. Check the store roster for local variations.
Q: How are damaged goods handled?
A: Scan the item as waste, place it in the marked bin, and tell the shift supervisor.
Q: How do I report a safety hazard?
A: Cordon off the area, then log it in the Safety app and notify your manager.
Q: Who do I contact for roster problems?
A: Message your store manager or email rosters@market.example.
